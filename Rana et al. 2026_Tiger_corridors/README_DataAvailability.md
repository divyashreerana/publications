The available data and code has been used in the manuscript titled "There is no silver bullet: Tiger corridors do not ensure multispecies carnivore connectivity" submitted to bioRxiv in July 2026.
bioRxiv doi: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.05.736645v1

The description of attached files are as follows.

Codes: 
1. preparing_spatial_data.Rmd/.html: codes to smooth rasters with moving window analyses for resistance surface optimization 
2. resistance_surface_optimization.Rmd: codes to identify optimal scale for landscape variables and to optimize multisurface resistance surface using ResistanceGA
3. analyzing_and_processing_optimized_resistance_surfaces: codes to generate resistance curves and compare them across species, and for processing optimized rasters for circuitscape input
4. multispecies_connectivity_analyses: codes to process circuitscape outputs to understand connectivity patterns, compare across species, identify corridors, and to test efficiency of optimized corridors compared to existing tiger corridors

Data: "species"_unique_genotypes.csv/.vcf - filtered multilocus data with unique individuals for each species; .asc/.prj files - uncorrelated landscape rasters used in landscape genetic analyses
